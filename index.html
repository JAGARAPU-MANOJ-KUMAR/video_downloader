Frequently Asked Questions

Why do high quality videos (1080p, 4K) take longer to download?

YouTube stores high-quality video and audio as separate streams. Our server downloads both and merges them into a single MP4 file using ffmpeg before sending it to you. This ensures you get proper video+audio in every quality.

Why do I see multiple audio tracks?

Many YouTube videos have multiple language audio tracks (e.g., English, Hindi, dubbed versions). The Audio Downloads section shows all of them separately so you can download whichever language you need.

The download starts but produces a corrupt file — what's wrong?

This usually means ffmpeg.exe is missing from the project folder. ffmpeg is required to merge video and audio streams. Make sure ffmpeg.exe is placed in the project root directory alongside yt-dlp.exe.

Is it legal to download videos?

Downloading is only legal for content you own, have explicit permission to download, or content that is in the public domain. Always respect copyright laws and the platform's terms of service. This tool is for personal use only.

Why is the video taking so long?

Large files (especially 1080p and above) take time to download and merge on the server. The download starts automatically once the server finishes processing. A typical 10-minute 1080p video may take 30–90 seconds to prepare.

Which video format is best?

MP4 with H.264 (avc1) is the most compatible format — it works in every media player, phone, and browser. AV1 and VP9 formats offer better compression but need a modern player. For audio, M4A (AAC) is the most compatible choice.
